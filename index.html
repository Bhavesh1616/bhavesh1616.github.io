HR Attrition Dashboard

Where does employee attrition concentrate, and which groups does it affect most?

  • Headline KPIs: 1,480 employees, average age 36.92, average income 6.50K, 7.01 average years at the company and a 9.22 average commute distance.

ECommerce Sales Dashboard

How do sales, profit and order ageing move across months and regions?
